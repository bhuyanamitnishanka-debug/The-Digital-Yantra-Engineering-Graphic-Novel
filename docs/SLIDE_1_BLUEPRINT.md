## 🎨 Visual Layout & Theme Variables

* **Background Canvas Color:** Parchment Cream (`#F7F4EB`)
* **Primary Structural Line Geometry:** Trace a crisp, 1px thin vector circle centered on the right half of the slide using Blueprint Cyan (`#00A8B5`) at 30% opacity. Intersect it with a vertical and horizontal crosshair line to mirror a CAD root grid.
* **Typography Archetype:** 
  * Headers: **Cinzel** or **Georgia** (Deep Ink `#1E242B`)
  * Structural technical text labels: **Roboto Mono** or **Courier New**
